Why I'm doing this

Eight years on the path. This is my way of giving back.

Aum K RaviArhatic Yoga practitioner, pranic healer, and the strategist behind Growth Arc

For eight years, Pranic Healing and Arhatic Yoga have been at the centre of my life, and the community around them has given me more than I can repay.

My working life has been in marketing. Seven-plus years inside the core marketing teams of Volkswagen Motorsport, Yamaha and Jeep India, then seven-plus years building digital funnels for founders and personal brands across India and Germany.

Somewhere between the two, it became impossible to ignore. So many gifted healers do genuine, beautiful work and stay invisible online. Bookings scattered across WhatsApp chats. Payments chased by screenshot. A centre that doesn't show up on Google Maps. An Instagram profile that says nothing about what they actually do. Not because they aren't good enough, but because technology keeps shifting under their feet, and it was never their calling.

So I'm putting the two halves of my life together. I build the website and set up everything around it, carefully and with you, so you can stay with the part only you can do: the healing.

Not as a business. As seva.In gratitude to Master Choa Kok Sui

What I set up with you

One organised home, and the pieces that make it work.

Everything is created in your name and stays yours. We do the account steps together on a call, so nothing is ever held by someone else.

Your own websiteThe centre of it all

A clean, mobile-first website on your own domain that lays out your practice the way a visitor needs it: who you are and your lineage, the healings you offer, courses and meditations, upcoming events, your products, what people say afterwards, and one clear way to book.

  • Healings & sessions
  • Courses & events
  • Shop for products
  • WhatsApp booking
  • Testimonials

RazorpayGet paid properly

Accept payments for sessions, courses and energised products without chasing screenshots. We open your Razorpay account together, go through KYC step by step, connect payments to your site, and write the privacy, terms and refund pages Razorpay asks for.

Google My BusinessBe found nearby

When someone searches for pranic healing near them, your centre should appear on Google and Maps. We claim and verify your listing, choose the right category, add your hours, photos and services, and link it to your website and booking.

Instagram, set up rightRe-set, not started over

Most healers already have an account. We reshape it so a new visitor understands you in five seconds: a professional profile, a bio that says who you help and where, a link to your website, highlights for sessions, courses and testimonials, and WhatsApp contact buttons.
